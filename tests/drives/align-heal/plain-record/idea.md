Add a "Recently viewed" strip to the top of our internal docs site.

When someone opens a document we should remember it, and show the last five
they opened as a horizontal row of small cards above the search box — title,
the section it lives in, and how long ago they looked at it. Clicking one
reopens it. If they have never opened anything, the strip should not appear at
all rather than showing an empty box.

Keep it simple and fast; this is a convenience, not a feature anyone will
configure.

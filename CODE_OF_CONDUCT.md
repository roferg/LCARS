# Code of Conduct

## Standard and commitment

LCARS adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The linked Covenant is the authoritative standard; the guidance below explains
its application here rather than reproducing its text.

Everyone participating should be able to contribute without harassment or
discrimination, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Contributors and maintainers
share responsibility for a welcoming, respectful community.

## Expected behaviour

Be considerate, listen to different perspectives, and give actionable feedback
about the work rather than attacking the person. Accept corrections, acknowledge
mistakes, and take responsibility for their impact. Respect boundaries and
privacy, and keep discussion constructive even when you disagree.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualised attention, trolling,
personal attacks, and publishing someone's private information without consent
are not acceptable. Neither are other behaviours that undermine a safe,
professional community. Disagreement about a technical choice is welcome;
intimidation is not.

## Scope

This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and
other community spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project
publicly, including official accounts, events, or appointed roles.

## Reporting and enforcement

**A private reporting contact is not set yet.** Until one is, this project is
published to be read and used rather than to invite contributions, and there is
no monitored channel for conduct reports.

If you need to raise something, open an issue asking for a private channel —
without the details in it — and a maintainer will open one. Do not post a
sensitive incident report in a public issue.

Describe what happened, where, and when, with relevant links or evidence where
safe. Do not include unnecessary personal information. Maintainers should
protect the reporter's privacy and share incident details only as needed to
investigate and respond. A complaint involving a maintainer should be handled
by an uninvolved community leader; if none is available, that limitation must
be disclosed rather than promising independent review.

Maintainers may remove harmful contributions, explain required changes, and
restrict participation. Responses follow the Covenant's graduated enforcement
guidelines: correction for an isolated inappropriate act, a warning for a more
serious or repeated incident, a temporary ban for sustained misconduct, or a
permanent ban for a pattern of serious abuse. Severity and impact determine
the response, and serious incidents need not pass through every step.
